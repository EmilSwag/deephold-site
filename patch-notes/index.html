What changed in the mine

Every player-facing update, newest first. This page is built from the same list the game shows in its own "What's new" panel.

September 2026

Honest ledgers

v0.360.3 · 26 Sept 2026

Collection, Bestiary and Achievements now count only what the Hold can give you today, so every total can be finished.

  • Gear and foes found only behind the sealed dungeon and raid gates leave the totals until those gates open again; anything you already logged stays yours
  • Achievements nobody can earn yet (dungeon clears and 5 / 10 / 25 rare finds) are set aside, not lost
  • Silk cord now weaves at Crafting 10, before the first robes that need it
  • Earth and deep wards unlock with the runes they are made from, at Runecrafting 25 and 82
  • Hunting-zone foes that also roam a dungeon now point you to their zone in the Bestiary

Kiln coin

v0.360.2 · 26 Sept 2026

Ember drakes drop a little more gold, so the Drake Kiln stays the best-paying solo delve.

  • Ember drakes drop 80–163 gold (was 77–159); the Drake Kiln pays about 5% more per hour than the Black Forge again
  • The same drakes in the Sundered Vault, the Long Descent and the Sundering Spire pay the same small bump

Honest coin

v0.360.1 · 26 Sept 2026

Until an item has traded, its Market value now rises with its tier, so deeper ore, bars and draughts are worth more than starter ones.

  • Iron ore is worth more than copper and a deep draught more than a minor one, even before anyone has traded them
  • Gold per hour on the gathering cards now climbs with the ladder instead of falling
  • The bronze seam swings slower to match its double haul of copper and tin ore

Full stride

v0.360.0 · 26 Sept 2026

Every foe now stands at full height and strikes for real, party hunts pay extra XP, spare gear can be traded, and five new gathering spots fill gaps in the skill ladders.

  • All sixty foes have new full-body models, from the training rat to the Sundering
  • Foes wind up, lunge and strike at you instead of bobbing in place, and you flinch when the blow lands, not before
  • A fallen foe stays down for a beat before the next one steps in
  • Hunt in the same zone and tier as a party mate for +10% combat XP each, up to +20%, and the hunt panel names who you hunt with
  • Spare gear can be traded: pick it in the trade window, and both sides see its condition and repairs left before confirming
  • New at Mining 8: the bronze seam, a copper ore and a tin ore with every swing
  • New fish: silverfin at Fishing 17 and the gulper at Fishing 77, each with its own cooking recipe
  • New tree: deepyew at Woodcutting 82, whose log fletches more adamant arrows per bar at Crafting 80
  • New herb: ashmoss at Mining 73, brewed at Brewing 78 into the Stoneskin Tonic, a defence draught

Company and coin

v0.359.0 · 25 Sept 2026

Trades keep both sides in the loop, a party sees who is ready, the new-player guide leads to the right fight, and the Market says plainly when your email still needs verifying.

  • The party panel shows who is ready for a group expedition, and a running expedition shows on the Home screen under Right now
  • A trade invite pops up with an Open trade button, and a banner keeps a waiting trade in sight
  • If a trade can't open, the window says why instead of staying blank
  • Your offer stays put when you close the trade window, and an empty window picks up a reopened trade by itself
  • Both sides see a trade complete or get cancelled right away, and a finished trade lists what you gave and what you got
  • Offering only gold no longer reads as Nothing offered
  • Profiles read as a clean ledger: no empty title line, your depth is your deepest expedition shaft, and the actions sit in one row
  • The guide's first-fight step shows its real goal, and Go hunt rats now takes you to the monster hunts
  • With an unverified email the Market stays open to browse, and its post buttons say you need to verify first
  • Too many requests at once or a lost connection get a clear message instead of Oops! Something broke
  • Monster levels and the last coloured tags became carved marks of the Hold, and listings say 1 rung, not 1 rungs
  • Ending a hunt leaves a short recap: time, kills, XP and gold
  • The zone you're hunting in says Hunting here, so a stray click no longer restarts the hunt and wipes its tally

A fight worth watching

v0.358.0 · 25 Sept 2026

Combat zones get a hunt ledger with a living level bar, drops you see land and drawn hit marks, small hits stop flashing like big ones, and leaving a hunt for a skill takes one click.

  • Every hunt keeps a ledger beside the fight: time hunting, kills, XP, gold, food left and deaths, each with its pace per hour
  • Your combat level bar sits under the fight, shows the XP left and roughly how long it will take, and bursts when you level up
  • Spoils show as item art, biggest stack first, and a fresh drop pops with its amount as it lands
  • Hits land on drawn marks: red for damage, steel for a miss, a star only for a truly heavy blow
  • A small hit no longer flashes the whole stage red: the flash and the flinch follow how much health the hit really takes
  • Auto-eat is a health gauge with a marker at your eat threshold, so you can see when your dwarf will eat
  • While you hunt, a skill card starts its work in one click: Stop hunt & start ends the hunt and gets to it
  • Expeditions say plainly when another activity is running and offer to stop it, instead of greying out the launch
  • Zone cards, party invites, the party leader, a confirmed trade and the daily reward use carved marks of the Hold instead of coloured tags
  • Dungeons rest for now: fight monsters in the combat zones, and a ready party is sent on a group expedition
  • Numbers use a clearer digit font, so a 3 no longer reads as an 8

Play with Steam

v0.357.0 · 24 Sept 2026

Launching from Steam now drops you straight into the Hold with no email to confirm, the guide always walks you through your first delve, and headings are easier to read.

  • The Breach and Deep Expeditions are open: the Adventures tabs no longer say Coming soon
  • The Breach tab now shows the rift itself above the boss bar
  • Your first launch from Steam creates your dwarf on the spot: no email, no password, and you can trade as soon as the Market opens for you
  • The guide now always shows your first delve, even when the Market opens while you are still busy mining
  • Headings use a clearer pixel font: a capital C no longer looks like an O, in English and in Russian
  • While you hunt in a zone, skill cards now offer Back to the hunt instead of sending you to collect a delve you never started
  • In a zone, the food button beside the fight shows the full name and how many you carry, instead of cutting both short

A Breach you can break

v0.356.0 · 24 Sept 2026

The Breach now sizes itself to the dwarves who fight it, a style swap mid-dungeon keeps the right bonus, and four recipes stop asking for materials far above their level.

  • Each new Breach is sized from the last one: if the hold broke it, the next is 25% tougher; if it held, the next matches what everyone dealt, plus a little
  • Swapping combat style during a dungeon run now gives the passive bonus of your new style instead of keeping the old one
  • Combat zones warn you as soon as your food runs out, not only when no food is set
  • Iron arrows now use barrowpine, steel arrows and the steel crossbow use ironbark, and the Courage Draft uses shadeleaf, so each recipe asks for materials of its own tier

Your skin, in every swing

v0.355.0 · 24 Sept 2026

The skins you own now dress your dwarf in full 3D, from the equipment screen to every fight and drill, and the Forgeborn greatsword finally swings with both hands.

  • All eleven skins now dress your dwarf in 3D: armour, regalia and a matching melee, ranged or magic weapon, on the equipment screen, in fights, in skill drills and on your profile
  • The Forgeborn greatsword gets its own two-handed cut: both hands on the grip, a high wind-up over the shoulder and a heavy blow that carries through
  • A skin only changes the look: your gear keeps its stats, and the weapon you really hold still decides whether you swing, shoot or cast
  • Skin bows draw a live string, skins with their own gauntlets cover your hands, and Unequip in the Market puts your dwarf back in the gear you wear

A rod on day one

v0.354.0 · 24 Sept 2026

New dwarves now start with a fishing rod, and the tutorial sends you into your first fight instead of a locked door.

  • Every new dwarf starts with a Fishing Rod, so you can fish from your first minute — and cook your catch over charcoal from the oak you chop
  • The sixth tutorial step now opens the Training Grounds for your first fight; it used to point at Expeditions, which are still locked
  • That step now tells you what the fight is for: safe loot and combat XP while the Market timer runs
  • The tutorial card always fits on screen now — the Market step's buttons no longer hide below the bottom edge

Wear what you earned

v0.353.0 · 24 Sept 2026

Skins you own can now be worn, and every dwarf who looks you up sees the one you picked — while the Hold finally sounds like itself, from the ring of a clean hit to the hum of each region.

  • Equip any skin you own from the Market, one at a time; it shows on your profile card, and Unequip puts your dwarf back in work clothes
  • A skin you have already earned now reads Ready to claim and is yours for free — the reward you reached no longer says Locked
  • Fights play recorded blows that land harder the heavier the hit, plus misses, kills and a deeper toll when a boss falls
  • A boss winding up a big strike or turning to a new phase now sounds a warning before it lands
  • Every region hums with its own ambience, fights and boss rooms carry their own music, and all of it follows your volume settings

Every strike day counts

v0.352.0 · 22 Sept 2026

Breach season rewards now go to the dwarves who keep showing up: strike on enough days for the title, colour and skin, while the season's best still earn the pet and the crown

  • Strike the season boss on any day for the title, on a quarter of the season's days for the name colour and on 60% of them for the skin (23 and 55 days in Ember), whatever your damage share
  • The seasonal pet goes to the top tenth of the season's strikers and the capstone title to the top hundredth, always at least one dwarf, settled once when the season closes
  • The season ladder shows how many strike days you have and how many each reward needs
  • Breach gold is unchanged and still follows your share of the damage

A sword in hand, a blade in the off hand

v0.351.0 · 21 Sept 2026

A bronze sword and a parrying dagger for every fresh dwarf, softer bites in the beetle hollows and the magma run, and a cheer for the first of each creature you fell in the open

  • Stone beetles, spore ticks, vein lurkers and the vein shade hit for less and fall sooner — those pools were eating far more food an hour than everything around them
  • A bronze sword is worn in the main hand and a bronze parrying dagger in the off hand on register, and each is handed back to any dwarf whose hand is empty, exactly like the crude pick and axe
  • Walking into a zone with no meal set now picks the best food you carry; a locked zone says which skill it counts, the level it wants and about how many kills away that is
  • A zone gate is judged on the weapon in your hand — a bow counts your ranged, bare fists your melee — and no tier is listed whose gate sits above level 99
  • The first kill of each creature in a zone is celebrated the way a delve's is, and every monster now wears the one bust the bestiary uses

Two hands on the haft

v0.350.0 · 15 Sept 2026

Every rung of the ranger's and the mage's kit has its own piece and its own bag icon, and your dwarf now swings the pick, the axe and the staff with both hands

  • Leather, hide, scale and drake for the ranger; robe, silk, runecloth and void for the mage; a parrying dagger per metal — each with its own look in the bag and on the dwarf
  • The pick is held near the butt with the other hand up the haft, the axe comes over the shoulder, the rod is steadied with two hands, the bow stands upright and the staff is thrust two-handed; the header figure trains with whatever weapon is in hand
  • A fresh dwarf is never bare: a linen tunic, wool trousers and leather boots fill every empty slot until better is worn

No hammer, no bars

v0.349.0 · 7 Sept 2026

The furnace stopped being an exception: smelting wants the hammer worn, the same as the anvil, and every dwarf now wears one

  • Every smelt — bronze through rune — asks for a smith's hammer in hand. Ore does not become a bar bare-handed any more than a seam becomes ore without a pick
  • The hammer is WORN on register now instead of sitting in the bag, and it is re-issued to any dwarf holding none, exactly like the crude pickaxe and axe
  • Forging a replacement hammer stays the one piece of smithing that needs no hammer — its own recipe is paid for in bronze bars, so gating it would have closed the loop for good

No pick, no ore

v0.348.0 · 5 Sept 2026

Every seam and every tree now wants a tool in hand — the Hold issues a crude pickaxe and axe to all, and your dwarf works the skill in the header, whole and in their own armour

  • Mining and woodcutting are strict from the first rung: copper, tin and oak want a pickaxe or an axe WORN, the same as iron and spruce always did — and put the pick down mid-swing and the swing stops
  • Every dwarf, new or old, is issued a crude pickaxe and a crude axe: tier 0, no speed, never smithed. Lose your last working one and the quartermaster hands you another at the gate
  • A card whose tool is sitting in your bag now says 'Equip' and puts it on for you instead of sending you shopping
  • The skill header shows your whole dwarf, soles to raised pick, in their own armour, standing on a plate that steps back a shade — a lighter build of the same body, drawn only while you can see it

The deep fights back

v0.347.0 · 2 Sept 2026

Seven combat zones that never stop, a passive tree with three roots, boots for every tier — and the deep now pays in coin

  • Seven combat zones open, from the Rat Warrens to the Horror Abyss — pick a zone and a tier and the fight never stops: you recover between kills, eat from your food slot when health runs low, and keep hunting while you are away
  • The Fence sets up shop in the Hold: he buys any stack, no questions asked, at a fifth of what the market says it is worth — or decomposes your junk into essence
  • A passive tree takes root with three branches — Skills, Trade and Combat — and twenty nodes to spend points on; every ten total levels earns a point, and a refund costs gold and a bar of the tier
  • Boots are their own armour slot now, with a pair to smith for every metal from bronze to rune — and Ranged and Magic finally have full ladders to level 90: bows, crossbows, staves and light armour, each staff tier burning its own rune
  • Combat rebalanced: armour now soaks damage — every landed hit is cut by your defence, diminishing and never immune — the deeper monsters hit harder to match, deeper delves pay more gold per hour the whole way up, and a wipe wears gear harder than a clear
  • Every action card shows xp, items and gold per hour, with a Loot & XP log for the session; each level past a recipe's requirement adds a 1% chance to finish it twice; the queue holds 30 actions (60 for Patrons) and takes 'until level' targets
  • Brew teas of wisdom, diligence, fortune and plenty for a tea slot that drinks itself when the buff lapses; Miner's Ale joins Brewing at level 7 and Barrowpine logs join Woodcutting at level 20
  • Delves from the Ashen Crawl down now take an entry stake — food, wards or drafts and a little gold; cooked fish finally look cooked on every plate; and new characters start with eight cooked meat against a training rat tuned for bare fists
