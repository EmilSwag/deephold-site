Quest: The first hour

Start point: this page. Requirements: none. Length: short. Most of it runs without you.

  1. Make a name. Pick a name and a look. Takes under a minute.

  2. Queue the work. Up to 30 actions: mine, smelt, smith, fight. They run one after another.

  3. Close the tab. Progress keeps going for up to 12 hours.

  4. Come back to the haul. Sell it on the market or smith it into something better.

Quest rewards

  • A queue that works while you sleep
  • Your first market sale
  • A reason to come back tomorrow

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The browser version is free to play. Patron is optional: longer offline time (72h instead of 12h), a bigger queue and +15% XP.

Does it really keep going when I close the tab?

Yes. Queue up to 30 actions and leave. Progress runs for up to 12 hours while you're away.

Are the other players real?
