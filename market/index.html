The rules

  • 5%tax on every sale
  • 1%listing fee
  • 8open orders (20 with Patron)
  • 7 daysbefore an order expires

How to trade

  1. Open the market tab in the game.
  2. List an item at your price, or buy from the cheapest seller.
  3. Sold items pay out while you're offline.
