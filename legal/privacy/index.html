Privacy policy

Last updated 4 Oct 2026

Deephold is run by YOUR-LEGAL-NAME ("we", "us"). This policy covers the game on Steam and in the browser, and this website. Questions or requests: privacy@YOUR-DOMAIN.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password we only store as a salted hash, or your Steam ID if you sign in with Steam, and whether your email is verified.
  • Game data: your character name, progress, inventory, trades, market orders, guild, friends and party.
  • Chat and reports: messages you send in game chat, and reports you file or that other players file about you, so moderators can act on abuse.
  • Security data: the IP address and time of sign-ins and of active sessions, used to keep accounts safe and to stop cheating and abuse. A session ends when you sign out or after 30 days without use.
  • Game events: basic events about how the game runs (for example, which screens load and when something fails), sent to our own servers so we can fix bugs and balance the game. We don't use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Purchases: if optional cosmetic purchases are offered, Valve (Steam) or Stripe handles the payment. We get an order record (item, price, status), never your card details.
  • Waitlist: if you leave your email on the waitlist, we store the address, the time, and which page the form was on. It is used for one message when the game opens, and at most one more if a test round opens before that; then the list is deleted. Ask at privacy@YOUR-DOMAIN any time and the address is removed the same day.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and the game you asked to play (contract).
  • To keep the game secure and fair, moderate chat, fix bugs and improve the game (legitimate interests; you can object, see "Your rights").
  • To keep purchase records that tax and accounting law requires (legal obligation).

We only email you about your account, for example to verify your address, or once about the launch if you asked for that on the waitlist. We don't send marketing email.

Who else handles it

We don't sell personal data or share it for advertising. These providers process data for us, only to run the service:

  • Hosting: our cloud hosting provider runs the game servers and the database.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: checks that sign-ups and sign-ins come from a person, not a bot. Cloudflare sees your IP address and browser details for that check.
  • Email delivery: an email service sends account emails such as address verification.
  • Waitlist storage: the waitlist form posts to a small service we run on Cloudflare Workers, so Cloudflare processes the address and your IP address for that request. If we move the list to an email-list provider, this page is updated first.
  • Valve (Steam): Steam sign-in and Steam purchases.
  • Stripe: card payments in the browser version, if offered.
  • Discord: only if you join our Discord server. Our Discord page loads Discord's public server widget (the online counter), which sends your IP address to Discord.
  • Google Fonts: this website loads its fonts from Google, so Google receives your IP address when you open a page.

How long we keep it

  • Account, game data and chat: while your account exists. We delete them when you delete your account.
  • Sessions: until you sign out or after 30 days without use.
  • Sign-in history: kept to protect your account, and deleted with it.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax law requires, usually up to 10 years.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to or limit how we use it, and complain to your local data protection authority. Email privacy@YOUR-DOMAIN from the address on your account, or include your Steam ID. We reply within 30 days.

Deleting your account: send the same kind of request. We delete your account and personal data within 30 days, except records the law makes us keep.

Children

Deephold is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you are below the age of digital consent where you live (up to 16 in parts of the EU), ask a parent or guardian before you play. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Local storage and cookies

The game keeps your sign-in tokens and settings in your browser's or the desktop app's local storage. The game needs them to work, and they don't track you on other sites. This website sets no cookies of its own.

Transfers abroad

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, such transfers are covered by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Security

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and only the people who need it to run the game can access your data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the game or on this site before the change takes effect.
