Questions people ask

Is it free?

Yes. The browser version is free to play. Patron is optional: longer offline time (72h instead of 12h), a bigger queue and +15% XP.

Does it really keep going when I close the tab?

Yes. Queue up to 30 actions and leave. Progress runs for up to 12 hours while you're away.

Where is the Steam version?

Coming. Join the Steam waitlist and you get one email the day the page opens.

Are the other players real?

Yes. The market, hiscores, guilds and the season boss are all shared with everyone online.
