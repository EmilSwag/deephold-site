About the game

Your dwarf keeps working when you close the game. Pick a job, set up gear and food, then leave — up to 24 hours of mining, forging or fighting pile up while you're offline. When you come back, you get a full report: resources gathered, levels gained and loot collected.

Twelve skills feed into each other. Mining, woodcutting and fishing bring in raw materials; smithing, crafting, cooking and brewing turn them into gear, food and potions; melee, ranged, magic and defence take that gear into combat. A pickaxe you forge helps you dig deeper; food you cook keeps you alive in a fight.

The economy belongs to the players. Almost nothing is sold by the game: every ore, bar and blade is made by someone and sold on an open market. You can buy, sell, place buy orders and trade directly. Market listing fees remove gold from circulation to keep prices stable.

Fights are shown in 3D. Watch your dwarf face goblins, spiders, drakes and more than fifty other monsters across seven zones and a two-hundred-floor Deep Delve. Every creature has its own model, animations and moves.

Stronger together. Join a guild, hunt in a party, take on server-wide world bosses like the Breach, or climb the seasonal leaderboards. Chat with other players in world and guild chat.

Cosmetics you earn by playing let you style your dwarf and your hall. No pay-to-win.

AI disclosure

The Deephold team used AI tools to help create some content, and every piece was reviewed, edited and approved by people. That includes the models, textures and animations for monsters and the player character, plus 2D art such as store images, icons, background illustrations and screenshot overlays. The music is AI-generated and was selected by us. The trailer was edited by hand from footage recorded in the game. The game's code, systems, balance, economy and main text were made by people, and the game does not generate any AI content while you play.
