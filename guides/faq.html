FAQ

Short answers to what new players ask most.

Is Deephold free?

Yes, it is free to play. Patron is optional: 60 queue slots instead of 30, 72 hours offline instead of 12, +15% XP, 20 market orders instead of 8 and 3 bank tabs instead of 1.

Does it keep going when I close the tab?

Yes. Queued actions and hunts keep running for up to 12 hours offline (72 with Patron), and the game settles everything when you come back.

Is there combat?

Yes: 7 hunting zones with 25 foes, from level 1 to 75. You pick the foe and the fight runs itself. See the hunting guide.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Party mates hunting the same zone and tier give each other +10% XP each, up to +20%. Guilds share a hall, a bank and a treasury. You can also trade directly with any player.

Is there a season boss?

Yes. Once a season a boss comes up through the Breach, and the whole server fights the same health bar. Right now it is The Ember Sovereign.

Which languages?

English and Russian.

Is there a Steam version?

Yes, a Steam client is on its way. Until its page is up, the waitlist is the way to hear when it lands.
