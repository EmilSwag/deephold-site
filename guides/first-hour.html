Your first hour

Deephold plays itself while you are away. The first hour is about setting it up so it keeps earning after you close the tab.

  • Fill the queue
  • Start with gathering
  • Cook before you fight
  • Your first hunt
  • Your first sale

Fill the queue

Everything in Deephold is an action on a timer: mine a vein, chop a log, cook a fish. Line up to 30 of them and walk away. The queue keeps running with the tab closed, for up to 12 hours offline, and the game settles everything the moment you come back.

Patron raises the queue to 60 and the offline window to 72 hours. Nothing in this guide needs it.

Start with gathering

Mining, Woodcutting and Fishing need nothing but time, and they feed everything else: ore becomes bars in Smithing, fish go to Cooking, logs go to Crafting. At level 1 you can already work:

  • Mining: Copper Ore, Tin Ore, Rune Essence, Deepleaf
  • Woodcutting: Oak Log, Charcoal, Sheaf of Grain
  • Fishing: Raw Cave Eel

The skills guide lists every action with its level, time and XP, so you always know where the next unlock is.

Cook before you fight

Hunts cost health. Food heals you mid-fight and auto-eat does the eating, so stock up on cooked food before your first hunt. A few minutes of Cooking now saves a long walk back later.

Your first hunt

Rat Warrens opens at level 1. The locals: Training Rat, Deep Rat. Pick a foe and your dwarf fights on its own, one kill after another, online or off.

Bring friends. Every party mate hunting the same zone and tier adds +10% XP, up to +20%. The hunting guide covers all 7 zones.

Your first sale

The market is player-run: every price on it is somebody's order. Selling and buy orders open after a short warm-up of 5 minutes of play, total level 12 and the first tutorial steps. They also need a verified email. Accounts made through Steam count as verified.

You start with 3 vouchers for buy orders, worth 500 gold each. Need gold right now? The fence buys anything on the spot at 20% of its going price. More in the market guide.
