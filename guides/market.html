Guide

Market and trade

No shop sells gear. Everything on the market was mined, crafted or looted by a player, and every price is somebody’s order.

  • Orders
  • Fees
  • The fence
  • Trading with a player

Orders

  • Place sell orders and buy orders. They match on their own, online or off.
  • Up to 8 open orders at once (20 with Patron).
  • Orders last 7 days. Whatever did not trade comes back to you.
  • Prices more than 10× away from the 7-day average are refused.
  • New players get 3 vouchers for buy orders, worth 500 gold each.

Selling and buy orders open after 5 minutes of play, total level 12 and the first tutorial steps, and need a verified email. Accounts made through Steam count as verified.

Fees

A 1% listing fee when you place an order and a 5% tax on every sale. Both leave the economy for good, which keeps gold worth something as the server grows.

The fence

The fence buys anything on the spot at 20% of its going price. Use it when you need gold now or nobody is bidding.

  • Up to 50 lines per sale.
  • A daily cap of 20,000 gold, plus 500 for every total level.

Trading with a player

Open a trade with anyone by name. Both sides put up gold, items and gear, and everything sits in escrow until both confirm. Gear shows its durability and repairs left before you accept.

Direct trades unlock once your account is 24 hours old and your total level reaches 15.
