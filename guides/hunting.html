Hunting and bestiary

Pick a zone, pick a foe, and your dwarf fights on its own. 7 zones from level 1 to 75, 25 foes, and what each one hits for and drops.

How hunting works

  • Hunts run on their own, online or off, like every other action.
  • Food heals you between blows, and auto-eat does the eating.
  • Melee, Ranged and Magic are trained by hunting, and so is Hitpoints.
  • Hunt beside your party in the same zone and tier: +10% XP per mate, up to +20%.
